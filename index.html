Experience

Research Assistant — CMU McLearn Lab

This summer, I worked as a Research Assistant at CMU's McLearn Lab under Prof. Bruce M. McLaren, developing and evaluating an LLM-powered Socratic dialogue tutoring system targeting students' decimal misconceptions.

TailorTab

TailorTab is a Chrome extension that surfaces search results tailored to a user's common search patterns and the time of day. It placed 3rd out of 1,300+ teams at the Mind the Product World Product Day Hackathon.

Zetheta Algorithms

At Zetheta Algorithms, I built an autonomous financial research agent that plans and executes multi-step research tasks, pulling from 10+ tools and a 3-layer memory system to limit hallucinations.

Riseva

Riseva is an AI-powered educational tool with 15 customizable parameters that lets teachers guide an LLM's output to shape student thinking, delivering personalized student responses and real-time insights into how students are reasoning. It placed 2nd out of 60+ teams at ProdHacks.
